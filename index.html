Why this book, why now

Everyone says "we want change." What we need first is clarity.

Shouting for change doesn't produce a strategy or a next step. A clear plea for clarity does. Without it, Bishop Carlton writes, life becomes long and heavy, and chaos turns into the lens our children inherit.

Without clarity, we find ourselves:

  • Taking job after job that doesn't fit who we are
  • Angry at people who aren't responsible for our stalled lives
  • Moving in and out of relationships without direction
  • Letting fear make decisions that faith should make

This book is his answer. When you finish it, you will know when you have clarity, and you will know when you don't.

Meet the author

A bishop who learned clarity the long way

Before the pulpit, Chadwick Carlton was a Division I-A college football player and a rising sports-business executive with a Major League Baseball team. A lost promotion, a near-fatal illness, and years of chasing the wrong dreams taught him what this book teaches: clarity is usually closer than we think.

Today he serves as a Bishop in the Church of God in Christ, with supervisory charge of churches in the Republic of Korea. He is a pastor, scholar, and life coach who writes at the meeting point of faith, history, and everyday life. He and his wife, Darice, are the parents of three.
