Strategy

Message, audiences, and channels

All copy comes from the manuscript. Say "Coming 2027" only, never a month.

Core message

  • Everyone says "we want change." What we need first is clarity: seeing better days through the smog of life.
  • The "winds" of life reveal what is stable and what must be surrendered.
  • Founding Readers get the founding price and a live Q&A with the Bishop.
  • The Companion Study Guide is printed inside the book.

Who we're reaching

  • Healing H2O congregation, Dulles, VA
  • COGIC network, including the churches in Korea
  • The Bishop's circles: University of Maryland football, sports business
  • Readers of centering prayer, Thurman, Keating, and stoicism
  • Small groups and Bible studies (13-session Study Guide)

Channels

  • Pulpit announcements and the church bulletin
  • Church table on Oct 25: buy one for you, one for a friend
  • Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, twice a week
  • Lobby poster and flyers
  • Email, text, and personal outreach from the Bishop

Phases

  • Team prep and buzz: Oct 17 to 24 (no sales)
  • Founding sale: Oct 25 to Nov 8, 2026
  • Build the list: Nov 9 until launch
  • Launch: 2027
  • After launch: reviews, study groups, speaking
